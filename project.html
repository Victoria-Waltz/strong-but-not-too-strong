The Project

The research behind Strong, But Not ‘Too Strong’.

Research Question

“What barriers, perceptions, and experiences shape women’s participation in public weight rooms?”

Project Goals

  • Explore the historical and cultural context of women’s strength training.
  • Examine common barriers women encounter in gym environments.
  • Discuss the relationship between fitness, confidence, and identity.
  • Consider the influence of social media and community.
  • Encourage thoughtful discussion about women’s experiences in fitness culture.
