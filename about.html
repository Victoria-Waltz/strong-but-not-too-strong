About the Podcast

Women, strength, and what it means to belong in the weight room.

Why the Weight Room?

Strong, But Not ‘Too Strong’ is a podcast mini-series about women’s experiences with lifting in public gyms and college fitness spaces.

The weight room can be a place to build confidence, challenge yourself, and feel part of a community. Stereotypes about who belongs there and what women’s bodies should look like can also shape how comfortable it feels to walk in, ask for help, or take up space.

The series explores how those experiences are shaped by fitness history, social media, and the people we lift alongside.

Meet the Creator

Victoria Waltz

Computer Science Student | Temple University Honors Program

My Story

Hi! I'm Victoria, a senior at Temple University studying Computer Science and a member of the Honors Program.

My interest in weightlifting actually started back in high school. During my junior year, I took a weightlifting class and went to the gym a few times with one of my friends. At the time, though, that was about as far as my lifting journey went. It wasn't until the summer before my sophomore year of college that I really started taking it seriously, and I've been lifting consistently ever since.

Growing up, I was always very skinny, and I wanted to change that. I wanted to build muscle, get stronger, and feel more confident in myself. Looking back, starting weightlifting was one of the best decisions I've made.

Even though I had some experience going into it, I was definitely nervous when I first started lifting in college. I was in a new environment, and while I had friends I could talk to about the gym, I spent most of my time training alone. Over time, I became more comfortable and confident, but I'd be lying if I said I never feel intimidated anymore. Even now, I still have those moments.

What Weightlifting Means to Me

Weightlifting has genuinely changed my life. Seeing the physical progress I've made has been incredibly rewarding, but the benefits go so much further than appearance. I've become more confident, and I've noticed such a positive difference in both my physical and mental health.

The gym has become a constant in my life. It's somewhere I go when life gets stressful, but it's also somewhere I love being when everything is going great.

Why I Created This Podcast

That's a huge reason why I chose this topic for my Honors Senior Project. Weightlifting has had such a positive impact on my life, and I want other women to have the opportunity to experience that same feeling.

I know how intimidating getting started can be, and I hope this podcast can help make strength training feel a little more approachable.

If there's one thing I want listeners to take away from Strong, But Not "Too Strong," it's that it's okay for women to be strong, too.

Weightlifting is about so much more than how your body looks. It's about building confidence, challenging yourself, and developing a healthier lifestyle.

Beyond the Gym

Outside of the gym, I'm also the President of Temple Club Tennis! I love playing golf, exploring the outdoors, finding new food and dessert spots, and spending time with my friends.

I'm excited to share something that's been such an important part of my life, and I hope this podcast encourages other women to discover what strength can mean to them.
