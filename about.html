About the Podcast

Women, strength, and what it means to belong in the weight room.

Why the Weight Room?

Strong, But Not ‘Too Strong’ is a podcast mini-series about women’s experiences with lifting in public gyms and college fitness spaces.

The weight room can be a place to build confidence, challenge yourself, and feel part of a community. Stereotypes about who belongs there and what women’s bodies should look like can also shape how comfortable it feels to walk in, ask for help, or take up space.

The series explores how those experiences are shaped by fitness history, social media, and the people we lift alongside.
