Research & References

Strong, But Not 'Too Strong' explores the experiences of women navigating weightlifting and fitness culture. Throughout the development of this Honors Senior Project, academic studies, historical books, and research from professional organizations helped shape the project's questions and discussions. These resources provide context for understanding women's experiences in the gym, including barriers to participation, body image, confidence, social expectations, and community.

How Research Shaped This Project

Developing Research Questions

Research about women's fitness participation, confidence, body image, and gender expectations helped inform the themes explored throughout the project.

Understanding Historical Context

Historical research provides background on how women's participation in fitness has evolved and how cultural expectations influence perceptions of strength.

Exploring Gym Experiences

Academic studies examine the challenges women can encounter in fitness spaces, including intimidation, judgment, equipment access, and feelings of belonging.

Connecting Research to the Podcast

Published research provides context for discussions about confidence, muscularity, community, and the meaning of strength. The podcast combines this research with personal reflections and clearly distinguished examples.
